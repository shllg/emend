# Pin a review to the top of the inbox

Issue #212. A long-running review gets buried when newer ones arrive. Pinning keeps it in reach.

## Behaviour

A reviewer pins an open review from its row in the inbox. A pinned review stays at the top of the
Open group until it is unpinned.

```mermaid
stateDiagram-v2
    direction LR
    [*] --> Open
    Open --> Pinned: pin
    Pinned --> Unpinned: unpin
    Unpinned --> Pinned: pin
    Pinned --> Retired: retire (clears the pin)
    Unpinned --> Retired: retire
```

## Limits

Each owner can pin at most ten reviews. The eleventh pin returns `409 pin_limit`.

## Out of scope

- Pinning from the phone's swipe actions.
- Ordering pins by hand.
- Pinned reviews in the Snoozed and Done groups.
