# Release notes for 0.9

## Highlights

- **Pinned reviews.** Pin an open review and it stays at the top of the inbox until you unpin, approve or retire it.
- **Drafts survive new revisions.** A comment you are still writing moves to the newest revision instead of being lost.
- **Faster diffs.** Large diffs open without blocking the page.

## Fixes

- Retiring a review no longer leaves stale snooze times behind.
- The phone layout keeps the Send button in reach when the keyboard is open.

## Upgrading

Run `emend update` on every machine. The database migrates on the first start, and old reviews open as before.
