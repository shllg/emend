# Plan: read reviews offline on the phone

Issue #211. A reviewer on a train opens a review and finds nothing. This plan lets the phone keep the
reviews it opened last.

## Goal

Reviews opened on the phone stay readable without a connection. Comments written offline wait on the
phone and are saved when the connection returns.

## Approach

- The service worker caches each opened review's revision and its blobs.
- Drafts are already kept in the browser; they only need to survive a restart of the app.
- A banner says when the phone shows saved data.

## Risks

- A cached review can be old. The banner must say so.
- The cache must stay inside the device and never leave it.

## Open questions

- How many reviews should the phone keep?
- Should a Send wait for the connection or refuse?
